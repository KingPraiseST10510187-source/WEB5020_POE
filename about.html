About Greenfield United Football Club

Our History

Greenfield United Football Club was founded in 2008 by a group of passionate football enthusiasts in Johannesburg who wanted to create a community space where young people could develop their skills and love for the game. What started as a single senior team has grown into one of the most respected community soccer clubs in the region, with 8 youth teams and 2 senior men's teams.

Mission, Vision & Values

To provide accessible, quality football coaching and development opportunities for young people regardless of their background, ability, or financial circumstances.

To be a leading community sports hub that nurtures talent, builds character, and unites the neighbourhood through the beautiful game of football.

  • Community: We believe football brings people together.
  • Inclusivity: Everyone is welcome at GUFC, regardless of skill level or background.
  • Development: We focus on player growth both on and off the pitch.
  • Respect: We promote fair play, sportsmanship, and respect for all.
  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in coaching and conduct.

We are based at Greenfield Park, Johannesburg, which features:

  • Two full-size grass football pitches
  • One artificial turf training area
  • Modern clubhouse with changing rooms
  • Equipment store and first-aid station
  • Secure parking area
