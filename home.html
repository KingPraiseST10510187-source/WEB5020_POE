About Our Club

Greenfield United Football Club (GUFC) is a community-based football club founded in 2008 in Johannesburg. We provide quality football coaching and development opportunities for young people and adults. With 8 youth teams and 2 senior men's teams, we welcome players of all skill levels to our soccer club.
